Fig. 6. Incremental prediction in the four primary cohorts and external transfer to PUCH. (a) Change in held-out AUC after adding each gene set to IFN-4. Predictions for each patient were averaged over five repetitions of stratified five-fold cross-validation. Bars show nominal 95% intervals from 500 outcome-stratified patient bootstrap resamples that repeated preprocessing and fitting. APM used seven genes in Riaz, Hugo and Liu and six in Gide. (b) Empirical receiver operating characteristic curves for the two frozen single-sample rank models in PUCH (14 responders and 41 nonresponders). Both models were fitted in the same 249 primary-cohort patients at ridge penalty λ = 1. The augmented model used the six common APM genes. The diagonal indicates chance discrimination. (c, d) Paired differences in AUC and Brier score, respectively, for IFN-4 plus APM6 minus IFN-4 alone in PUCH. Each pair of models was fitted at the indicated penalty in the primary cohorts. The filled point marks the original penalty. Bars show nominal 95% intervals from 5,000 outcome-stratified PUCH bootstrap resamples with each pair of fitted models held fixed. Higher AUC and lower Brier score indicate better performance.
