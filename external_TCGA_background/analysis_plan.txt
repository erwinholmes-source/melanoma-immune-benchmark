TCGA-SKCM external expression definition of the CD8A-correlated background

This exploratory comparison was specified for version 21 after the original cohort results and earlier reference distributions were known, before computing the new distributions. The historical 733-gene universe fixes cross-platform coverage. Match these candidates to the cBioPortal TCGA PanCancer Atlas SKCM RSEM matrix by their archived Entrez IDs. Use all RNA-profiled metastatic specimens identified in the source sample table, one per patient, without survival criteria or response labels. Keep unique, finite, nonconstant expression for CD8A and candidate genes. Qualify a gene if its Spearman correlation with CD8A is at least 0.50. The index CD8A is eligible in the original and external definitions. Use the original (142) pool as the comparison.

Draw 10,000 four-distinct-gene sets uniformly within each of three pools: the TCGA-selected subset of the 733, the same subset after removing the four index genes, and the historical 142 after removing the four index genes. For comparison with the original estimates, retain the original percentile convention and outcome definitions. One deterministic seed (20261124) is used per new pool, gene order lexicographic. Every draw is assessed in the same four primary anti-PD-1 cohorts. Gene z-scores use all baseline patients within cohort. Positive direction is higher expression for response. Within a cohort count ties as one half; summarize each draw by arithmetic mean AUC across the four. Compute the observed rank percentile (half weight to ties) and upper-tail empirical P = (1 + number at or above observed)/(1 + 10,000). Preserve all three results regardless of direction. Validate the original 142-gene comparator versus the archived cohort-level ranks, and verify a subset of new draws using direct response/nonresponse pair comparisons. Report the patients, numbers of genes, index membership, and overlap with the earlier pool. TCGA is external for the definition of coexpression; response evaluation still uses the original cohorts.
